About This Page

Scroll down to see how each position property works. The colored boxes explain the different types of positioning. The right side demonstrates how z-index controls overlapping.

◆ 1. Sticky Position

Sticky elements remain in their normal position until they reach a specified scroll point.

◆ 2. Relative Position

Relative positioning moves an element from its normal position without removing its space.

◆ 3. Absolute Position

Absolute positioning places an element relative to its nearest positioned ancestor.

◆ 4. Fixed Position

Fixed elements stay in the same place on the screen even when you scroll the page.
